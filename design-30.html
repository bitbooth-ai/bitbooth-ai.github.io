1First floor · the character suite

Guests with faces

The hotel's most requested residents: macarons piped with bear, lemon and seasonal personalities. Each face is drawn by hand at the front desk, which is also the kitchen, which is also the ballroom.

2Second floor · the treasure suite

Small treasures

Look choup — fruits sculpted in mung-bean marzipan and glazed to a shine — and rose pia, pastry pleated into petals. Housekeeping refuses to tidy them away.

Guests are advised these mangoes are a beautiful lie. The gasp is included in the rate.

3Third floor · the garden suite

The rooftop garden

Buttercream peonies, dahlias and roses, piped petal by petal. The only hotel garden that doubles as dessert and triples as a centrepiece.

Arrives like a florist's window; leaves like a very good party. Vase not required.
