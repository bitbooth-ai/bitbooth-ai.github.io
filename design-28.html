Treasury · I

The Fruit That Isn't

Look choup: tiny fruits sculpted from mung-bean marzipan, painted by brush and sealed in glass-bright glaze. In the old tradition, sweets were shaped as fruit so that generosity would look as good as it tasted. We keep the tradition; the giggle when a guest bites a "mango" is complimentary.

Treasury · II

The Rose, Folded

Rose pia — pastry pleated into petals, one fold at a time, the way patient hands have always turned flour into flowers.

Treasury · III

The Court of Characters

Macarons with hand-piped faces — bears, lemons and honoured seasonal guests. Each expression is painted individually, which is why every box arrives in a slightly different mood, all of them good.

Treasury · IV

The Everlasting Garden

Buttercream peonies, dahlias and roses, raised petal by petal on the flower nail. Flowers as offerings — except these are offered to your dessert plate.
