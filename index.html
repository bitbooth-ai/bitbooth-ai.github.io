Cheeky Bite —
twelve design directions

One bakery, twelve candidate websites. Same photography, same products — character macarons, Thai sweets, buttercream flower cakes. Eight earlier directions were culled (dark themes, text-over-photo readability, joyless palettes — see PRODUCT.md for reasons). The third batch (16–20) throws away the shared skeleton entirely: a board game, an operating system, a swatch book, a field guide, a chat thread. Everything below is light, joyful, dark-on-light readable, and built for phones. Click through and pick a lane.

Sugar Rush

Candy-pop maximalism. Hot pink, stickers, marquee ticker, chunky type, desserts with attitude. Loud on purpose.

Pressed Flowers

Lilac scrapbook romance. Taped polaroids, handwritten notes, a keepsake album from the baker's kitchen table.

 → 11

Butter & Sun

Lemonade-stand yellow drench. Rotating sun rays, scalloped frosting dividers, alternating rows, "call me back" order card.

 → 12

The Bake Sale

County-fair gingham and blue-ribbon rosettes. Tilted plates, prize-tag recipe cards, ring-the-bell order form.

 → 13

The Sweet Market

A Bangkok morning-market stall, mango-bright. Striped awnings, hand-lettered price tags, Thai script woven through.

 → 14

The Ribbon Box

Mendl's-style powder-pink pastry box tied with a blue ribbon. Centered symmetry, script flourishes, gift-note order dialog.

 → 15

The Funny Pages

Sunday-comics edition where the macarons talk. Halftone panels, speech bubbles, a clip-out order coupon.

 → 16

Sugar Trail

A board game you play by scrolling. A macaron token travels a winding path past numbered product stops; the order form is the FINISH prize, with confetti.

 → 17

CheekyOS

The bakery as a pastel retro operating system. Boot splash, draggable windows, folders of photos, a dock, and Order.app as a system dialog.

 → 18

The Buttercream Color System

Products catalogued as an edible Pantone swatch book. Fanned chip deck, spec codes you can copy, "mix your palette" order form.

 → 19

Field Guide to Buttercream Flora

A Victorian naturalist's taxonomy — Paeonia cremora, Ursus macaron — with numbered plates, hand annotations, and a stamped Specimen Request Form.

 → 20

Sweet Talk

The whole site is a chat thread with the baker. Photo messages with tappable reactions, a voice note, quick replies, and an in-chat call-me-back card.
