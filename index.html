Cheeky Bite —
seven design directions

One bakery, seven candidate websites. Same photography, same products — character macarons, Thai sweets, buttercream flower cakes. Eight earlier directions were culled (dark themes, text-over-photo readability, joyless palettes — see PRODUCT.md for reasons). Everything below is light, joyful, dark-on-light readable, and built for phones. Click through and pick a lane.

Candy-pop maximalism. Hot pink, stickers, marquee ticker, chunky type, desserts with attitude. Loud on purpose.
