quill. logos

Eight directions, compared at favicon and display sizes on both surfaces.

Brief. Quill turns whatever is on the clipboard (Word, Outlook, Google Docs, Excel, VS Code, web pages) into clean Markdown. The mark should feel crisp, professional and modern, in line with BitBooth's work for investment teams. Constraints shared by every direction:
  • Palette limited to Quill's tokens: navy #15253E, silver #F9F7F8, purple #7B68DF, light-blue #99D0F2. Purple is the hero.
  • Few shapes, strong silhouette; must survive 16px as a favicon and look considered at 256px.
  • Self-contained on a rounded tile so the mark is identical on light and dark surfaces.
  • Hand-written SVG, viewBox 0 0 64 64, no fonts, no raster, no filters.

Nib & Hash 01quill-01-nib-hash.svg

Direction. A face-on fountain-pen nib whose slit and breather hole are formed by a Markdown #: writing instrument and output format in one glyph.

Rationale. Silver nib on a purple tile, with a bold four-bar hash cut out in tile purple and a short slit continuing to the tip so it still reads as a nib when the hash softens at small sizes.
