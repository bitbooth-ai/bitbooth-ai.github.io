The things I make

Macarons with personality

Every shell rests overnight, and every face is piped freehand the next morning — bears with sleepy eyes, lemons mid-laugh, a December parade of santas and snowmen. No two are ever quite the same, which is exactly the point.

Custom characters for birthdays, favours and people who need cheering up.

☞ makes: joy, roughly two dozen pieces at a time

smells like roses. is cake.

Sweets from Thailand

Look choup are tiny fruits sculpted from sweetened mung bean and dipped in glossy jelly — a craft from the old royal kitchens that I refuse to let become a factory thing. Rose pia is flaky pastry under a crown of rose-scented cream petals.

Both are made in small batches, painted by brush, and gone by Sunday.

☞ secret ingredient: a grandmother's patience

yes, the flowers are frosting

Gardens made of buttercream

Peonies, dahlias, ranunculus and roses — each one piped petal by petal on a spinning nail, chilled until firm, then lifted into place with warm scissors. A bouquet cake takes a full day of piping before assembly even starts.

For weddings, showers, and anyone who deserves flowers they can also eat.
