Cheeky Bite · Table for Everyone

A Tasting of
Small Happinesses

seven courses, one pair of hands

Served daily · From a very small kitchen

I

The Characters

Macarons with faces and opinions. Bears, lemons, and whoever else asks nicely. Each expression piped by hand, which is why no two are in the same mood.

Pairs well withSunday mornings and people who talk to their food.

II

Fruit, But Make Believe

Look choup — tiny fruits sculpted from mung-bean marzipan, painted and glazed until the mangoes blush. An old craft of making generosity look as good as it tastes.

Pairs well withGuests who say "wait, that's not a real mango?"

III

The Rose Course

Rose pia — flaky little cakes folded into petals. The kind of pastry you photograph first and apologise to later.

Pairs well withHot tea and unhurried afternoons.

"Palate cleanser: a look inside the kitchen. Yes, every face is piped by hand. Yes, sometimes I redo an eyebrow four times."

IV

The Garden

Buttercream peonies, dahlias and roses, piped petal by petal. Flowers that skip the vase and go straight to dessert.

Pairs well withBirthdays, apologies, and no reason at all.

V

The Bouquet

A full flower-box cake — an armful of blooms you're allowed to eat. Arrives looking like a florist's window; leaves looking like a good party.

Pairs well withAnnouncements. Any kind.

VI

Something Blue(berry)

A soft roulade, rolled around cream and blueberries. The quiet course. Still gone first, somehow.

Pairs well withSecond helpings.

VII

The Call

The final course is served by telephone. Leave your details and the baker will call you back to plan something sweet — a set, a bouquet, a whole celebration.

Very good. The baker will call you shortly. ❦

Sweet as art, served with a grin. Cheeky Bite · handmade · no substitutions on joy
