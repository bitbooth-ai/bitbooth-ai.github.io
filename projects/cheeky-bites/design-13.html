Two kitchens, one pair of hands

The look choup technique came from Thai grandmothers who measured nothing and got it perfect every time. The macarons and buttercream came later, from French books read past midnight. Somewhere between Bangkok and buttercream, this stall happened.

Everything is made in small batches, by one person, with the patience both traditions demand.

The flower cart

Buttercream peonies, dahlias and roses — piped petal by petal, sold by the bunch or planted on a cake.
